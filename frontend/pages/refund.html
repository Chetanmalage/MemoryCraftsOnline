Refund & Return

Our policy for returns, refunds and damaged items.

Return Policy

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for damaged or incorrect items. To be eligible, the item must be unused and in its original packaging.

Damaged Items

If your product arrived damaged, please take photos of the item and packaging, and contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

Refund Process

Once your return is approved, refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Non-Returnable Items

Personalized and customized products cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.
